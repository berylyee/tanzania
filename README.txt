TANZANIA TRAVEL SCRAPBOOK WEBSITE

1. Keep this entire folder together.
2. Open index.html in a browser to use the scrapbook.
3. Click a day on the map or a day card to open its page.
4. Click the “My experience” area to type your story.
5. Add/remove animal tags.
6. Add photos using the photo slots.
7. Your text and tags are saved in the browser's local storage.
8. “Export journal” creates a text backup of your written content.

The route map used as the visual template is in assets/tanzania-route-map.png.
